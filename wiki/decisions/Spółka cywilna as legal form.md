# Spółka cywilna as legal form

**Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** decided, not registered · **Domain:** [[Business]]

## Decision

[[Greg]] and [[Greg's wife]] run Projekt Wania as a spółka cywilna (s.c.).

## Why

Chosen after researching partnership options in Poland. _Reasons and alternatives considered: not recorded yet._

## What it means

General facts about a spółka cywilna; an [[Accountant]] should confirm them for our case.

- Each partner needs a CEIDG entry; the spółka gets its own NIP and REGON.
- A written umowa spółki sets shares, contributions and who can sign.
- Partners are jointly liable with their personal assets.
- Each partner pays tax on their share of profit. See [[VAT and tax]].
