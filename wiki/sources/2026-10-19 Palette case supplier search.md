# 2026-10-19 Palette case supplier search

## What it is

Greg's draft inquiry email to palette case manufacturers (original in `raw/2026-10-19 palette case inquiry email.md`), plus a web search by Claude for manufacturers of empty plastic eyeshadow palettes, Europe first. Done in the project thread "Producent pustych palet".

## What it says

**The inquiry (Greg's spec):**

- 9 shades (3x3) or 12 shades (3x4), quote both.
- Round pans, diameter still blank in the draft. The [[Producer]] fills and glues the pans, so cases ship empty.
- Large mirror inside the lid, magnetic closure preferred.
- Sturdy plastic (ABS, AS, PETG or acrylic, asks for the price difference), matte or semi-matte, Pantone colour.
- Logo: hot stamping, silk screen or embossing.
- Asks for MOQ and unit prices at 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 pcs, stock vs custom mould and mould cost, stock and custom samples, lead times, shipping (EXW / FOB / DDP), payment terms, AQL, REACH documents, an outer paper box, recycled plastic.

**What the search found** (each fact from the supplier's own website or a trade-press article, read 2026-10-19; nothing is confirmed by the suppliers yet):

- European palette production is mostly large packaging groups. They have stock (catalogue) palettes, which avoid a mould, but publish no MOQs.
- [Toly](https://www.toly.com/en/contact/): factory in Malta (EU), sales offices in France, Belgium, UK. Stock 12-pan palettes, e.g. the "Upstage" palette, with mirror or clear lid. Contact through the form on the contact page.
- [HCP Packaging](https://www.hcpackaging.com/contact/): stock and custom compacts and palettes. European offices in London, Paris, Munich; a German plant; most production in China. Contact through the enquiry form.
- [Quadpack](https://www.quadpack.com/): headquarters Barcelona, standard and custom make-up packaging, has shown a 10-pan ABS palette with mirror. No public contact details on the page read.
- [Euromakeup](https://www.euromakeup.it) (make-up arm of Eurovetrocap, Milan area, Italy): "flexible MOQs", lists an eyeshadow category. info@euromakeup.it.
- Lumson / Marino Belotti (near Bergamo and Crema, Italy): prestige powder compacts, not confirmed for multi-pan palettes. Likely high MOQ.
- China, plastic: [Zhejiang Weili Plastic](https://www.zjwlsj.com/) (Jinhua, manufacturer, palettes with mirror and magnetic closure, sales5@zjwlsj.com); [Shantou Tongzhuang Cosmetic](https://stkaqili.en.made-in-china.com/) (Shantou, magnetic empty palette listed at 0.53 USD FOB, MOQ 12,000, inquiry form).
- China, paper/cardboard palettes (different material than the spec): [Colorich](https://www.colorichpkg.com/products/eyeshadow-palette-packaging/) (Zhongshan, MOQ 500, admin@colorichpkg.com); [Major Custom Packaging](https://www.mcpackagings.com/product/eyeshadow-palette/) (Shenzhen, MOQ 1,000, inquiry@majorcustom.com).
- Not a fit: Faca Packaging (Barcelona) lists only jars and dispensers; A Packaging Group (US) has a 12-pan ABS palette with glass mirror but MOQ 10,000 at 5 USD.

**Correction, same day:** [[Greg]] found that none of the European names actually offers eyeshadow palette cases. Rechecked: Euromakeup, Quadpack, HCP and Lumson do not show palettes on the pages read, so they were dropped. Toly does: its showcase lists eyeshadow palettes made for Lancôme (12 shades), Armani and Clarins. Texen Beauty Partners (France) makes refillable palettes but sources products in Asia and North Africa ([Premium Beauty News](https://www.premiumbeautynews.com/en/texen-beauty-partners-expands-in,23110)). Added [SN Packs](https://www.snpacks.com/collections/eyeshadow-packaging) (Wuhan, China: custom magnetic plastic palettes 1-16 pans, info@snpacks.com).
