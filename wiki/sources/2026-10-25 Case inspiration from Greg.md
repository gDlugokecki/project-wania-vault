# 2026-10-25 Case inspiration from Greg

## What it is

Seven pictures of existing eyeshadow palettes that [[Greg]] sent on 2026-10-25 as inspiration for how our case could look. They are other brands' products, for reference only. Originals: `raw/inspiration/case/`.

What the examples have in common (Claude's reading, not Greg's words): a slim case, a full mirror in the lid, and mostly metallic finishes (gold, rose gold, champagne, silver). Five of the seven use square pans; our [[Producer]]'s standard pan is round Ø26 mm.

Open questions:
- Example 6 has a frosted see-through lid. Does that count against "nothing transparent" ([[Packaging]])?
- Are square pans an option? The [[Producer]] has other pan shapes, so a square-pan case may be possible.
- Is a metallic finish what Greg wants, or matte (as in the inquiry)?
