# 2026-10-30 EU plastic packaging and import research

## What it is

Web research by Claude on 2026-10-30, asked by [[Greg]] after a case supplier asked "Isn't plastic banned in Europe?". Two questions: is plastic restricted for cosmetic packaging in the EU, and what does importing palette cases from China to Poland involve. Result: [[Plastic packaging rules and import]].

## What we learned

- Plastic cosmetic packaging is not banned. PPWR (Regulation (EU) 2025/40) applies from 12 August 2026 and adds recyclability (grade C from 2030, B from 2038) and recycled-content targets (10% / 25% for contact-sensitive non-PET plastic in 2030 / 2040; cosmetics packaging is contact-sensitive per recital 21).
- The cosmetic safety report must cover the packaging material (purity, stability, migration); no fixed test list.
- EU duty: 3923 10 90 at 6.5% or 4202 12 50 at 5.2%; classification not settled. €150 duty-free limit gone since 1 July 2026 (flat €3 per item for low-value parcels until 1 July 2028).
- Import VAT can be settled in the VAT return under art. 33a by active VAT payers using a customs representative. EORI needed.
- Brand owners are packaging introducers in Poland: BDO registration (200 zł for a micro-enterprise), yearly report by 15 March; under 1 tonne a year the recycling and product-fee duties fall away with de minimis papers.
