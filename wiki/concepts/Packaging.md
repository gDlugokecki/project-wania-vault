# Packaging

The palette case and its design.

## Our situation

- Looking for a plastic case; no [[Case supplier]] chosen yet. Shortlist on that page. ([[Product]])
- Must fit the producer's pans. The [[Producer]] fills and glues the pans, so the case is delivered empty, to the producer.
- Design comes from the [[Branding agency]] and must carry the compliance label. ([[Brand]], [[Compliance and CPNP]])

## Case spec (from the inquiry email, 2026-10-19)

- 9 shades (3x3) or 12 shades (3x4).
- Round pans. **Diameter not known yet**: ask the [[Producer]] before sending the email.
- Large mirror inside the lid; magnetic closure preferred.
- Sturdy plastic (ABS, AS, PETG or acrylic), matte or semi-matte, Pantone colour.
- Logo by hot stamping, silk screen or embossing.
- Quotes asked at 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 pcs; samples before ordering; REACH documents; optional outer paper box; recycled-plastic options.
