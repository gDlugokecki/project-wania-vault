# Compliance and CPNP

EU rules for selling cosmetics: a safety assessment (CPSR), a CPNP notification, and correct labels with the INCI ingredient list.

## Our situation

- The [[Producer]] delivers all EU compliance; we pay for it. ([[Product]])
- Which documents exactly, and when we get them: open.
- Who acts as the EU "Responsible Person" (the producer or our spółka): open. ([[Business]])
- The safety report (CPSR) must also cover the case material; we get the documents from the [[Case supplier]]. Plastic is allowed. See [[Plastic packaging rules and import]] (2026-10-30).
- The label must fit the [[Packaging]] design. ([[Brand]])
