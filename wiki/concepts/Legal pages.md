# Legal pages

Pages a Polish online shop must have: privacy policy, regulamin (terms of service), returns and withdrawal policy, cookie policy.

## Our situation

- Cookie policy: exists in [[bvt-ecommerce]].
- Privacy policy: exists, with "DO UZUPEŁNIENIA" placeholders for company data, data categories, legal bases, processors, retention, user rights.
- Regulamin and returns page: missing.
- Need company details from [[Company registration]]. ([[Website]], [[Business]])
