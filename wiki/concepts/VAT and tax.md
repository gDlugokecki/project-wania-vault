# VAT and tax

## Our situation

- VAT payer or exemption threshold: open, ask the [[Accountant]]. ([[Business]])
- Import VAT on the cases from China (23%) is recoverable only as an active VAT payer, which also allows settling it in the VAT return (art. 33a) instead of paying at customs. See [[Plastic packaging rules and import]] (2026-10-30).
- In a spółka cywilna each partner pays tax on their share. ([[Spółka cywilna as legal form]])
