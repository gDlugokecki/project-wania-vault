# Store design prototype

## What it is

A high-fidelity design of the webshop, made by [[Greg]] in Claude Design. The project is "Girly Makeup Store Design": https://claude.ai/design/p/f428c94e-5188-49aa-bf07-3ab24fa7883c

- Working brand name in the design: "lula." (a placeholder, see [[Brand name]]).
- Design of record: `Lula Store.dc.html`. Developer handoff with design tokens: `design_handoff_lula_store/`.
- Covers the landing page, product page, cart and checkout, confirmation, login, register and account, on desktop and mobile, plus seven transactional email templates.
- Product data, prices, copy and photos are placeholders.
