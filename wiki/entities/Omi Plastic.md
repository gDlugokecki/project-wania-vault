# Omi Plastic

Dongyang Omi Plastic Technology Co., Ltd., China. Alibaba store `ominatural`. One of the [[Case supplier]] candidates for the [[Packaging]].

## Snapshot

- **Status:** rejected for now: MOQ 10,000 is firm, they cannot do 2,000 (2026-10-26)
- **Where:** Dongyang, China
- **Contact:** Kevin Jiang, Alibaba chat
- **Next step:** none unless we order 10,000

## What they can provide

- Logo printing / customisation possible (2026-10-26).
- **MOQ 10,000 pcs** (2026-10-23, repeated 2026-10-25). Far above our first order of 1,000-2,000. On 2026-10-25 they said they would check 2,000 units; on 2026-10-26 their manager refused: production only at 10,000.
- Four stock moulds offered (2026-10-23), no prices yet; a quote follows if we pick one:

| Model | Size (mm) | Wells | Lid | Mirror | Fits Ø26 pans? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3047 | 105 x 105 x 15 | 9 round, Ø27.5 | clear AS/ABS | no (clear lid) | possibly, but out: clear lid (Greg, 2026-10-23) |
| 3025 | 122 x 101 x 12 | 9 round, Ø28 | ABS, black | yes | possibly, needs fitting check |
| 3011 | 122 x 101 x 13 | 12 round, Ø24.5 | clear AS | no | no, wells too small; also clear lid |
| 3012 | 122 x 101 x 13 | 12 round, Ø24.5 | ABS | yes | no, wells too small |

Base and tray are ABS on all four. Drawings in `raw/suppliers/omi-plastic/`.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-23 models 3047 and 3011 are out -> clear lids; Greg wants nothing transparent ([[Packaging]]).

## Open questions

- They ask for our preferred style, colour/finish and logo file (2026-10-25). We have no logo yet ([[Brand name]]).
- Will they go below MOQ 10,000 for a stock mould?
- Price, sample cost and lead time for 3025 (the closest match: 9 wells, mirror).
- Can the 3025 wells be sized to our filled Ø26 pans, and is the closure magnetic?
- Their first reply came within seconds of the inquiry and looks automatic. They have not answered the other questions (REACH, shipping, payment, QC) yet.
