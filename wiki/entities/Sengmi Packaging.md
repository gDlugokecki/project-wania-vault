# Sengmi Packaging

Guangzhou Sengmi Packaging Product Co., Ltd., China. One of the [[Case supplier]] candidates for the [[Packaging]], contacted by Greg on 2026-10-29.

## Snapshot

- **Status:** quote for C64: 2.39 USD x 2,000 (custom Pantone, matte, no print), but wells Ø21 mm, too small; say they can make 22x22 or Ø26 mm (how, not clear)
- **Where:** Guangzhou, China
- **Contact:** Meng Smith, Alibaba chat
- **Next step:** Greg said (2026-11-03) he would check with his producer and come back; C64 wells Ø21 mm are too small, so only worth it if they make bigger wells

## What they can provide

As they state it, 2026-10-30:

- **C64 magnetic palette**, 9 round wells, mirror, existing mould (photo: [[raw/suppliers/sengmi-packaging/2026-10-30 C64 magnetic 9 round wells mirror.png|2026-10-30 C64 magnetic 9 round wells mirror.png]], shiny metallic green). **Inner well diameter 21 mm**, so it does not take our Ø26 mm round pans.
- MOQ 5,000 pcs on the existing mould.
- Logo by hot stamping, silk screen or embossing; customised sample with logo possible.
- Sample: 2 pcs, shipping about 65 USD (DAP). Incoterms EXW / FOB / DDP.
- Pre-production sample 7-15 days; mass production 30-35 days.
- Says they have exported this palette to the EU. Material documentation costs an extra certification fee.
- Can make an outer paper box. Not recyclable.
- Catalogue image: 3 series (magnetic, snap-in, clear-top), 12 models for private label, square palettes with mirror in 1, 4, 6, 9-well and other layouts ([[raw/suppliers/sengmi-packaging/2026-10-29 catalog 3 series 12 models.png|2026-10-29 catalog 3 series 12 models.png]]). Clear-top is out for us (no transparent).
- 2026-10-30: their biggest pan is 22x22 mm (example photo `2026-10-31 example pan in 2-well palette.png`). No stock 9-well palette for 22x22 (2026-11-02).
- 2026-11-02: asked "can you make 22x22 mm or Ø26 mm?": "Yes, we can, how many you need". Not said whether this needs a new mould.
- 2026-11-03: **quote for C64** 9-colour magnetic palette (wells Ø21 mm), top, middle and base in our Pantone, matte, no printing: **15.53 RMB = 2.39 USD per piece x 2,000 = 4,780 USD**, ex shipping, taxes and customs; valid 10 days. Confirmed again that only pans up to 21 mm fit C64.
- 2026-11-02: offered 1 sample of C64 to test; wants an address for shipping.
