# Sambound Packaging

Sambound New Material Technology Packaging Limited, Alibaba store `cosmeticpackagingvn` (location not recorded). The catalogue file name suggests the brand Chengbang (诚邦). One of the [[Case supplier]] candidates for the [[Packaging]], contacted by Greg on 2026-10-29 on Alibaba.

## Snapshot

- **Status:** best lead so far: YR3091, a 4x2 (8-well) palette with square wells about 22.5 mm that Lacy says fits a 22x22 pan; no price yet. YR3121A (3x3) wells too small; a custom grid would cost 4,000-5,000 USD mould
- **Where:** not recorded
- **Contact:** Lacy Luo, Alibaba chat
- **Next step:** Greg to ask YR3091 price at 2,000, magnet and sample cost. Draft: [[raw/suppliers/sambound-packaging/drafts/2026-11-09 reply|drafts/2026-11-09 reply]]

## What they can provide

- 2026-10-30: none of their existing public-mould products fit 9 (3x3) or 12 (3x4) round aluminium pans.
- 2026-10-30: sent a catalogue of their public moulds (PDF, 27 MB), not downloaded.
- 2026-10-31: product card, 9-pan palette with mirror, 2.40-2.60 PLN, MOQ 5,000 (`2026-10-31 product card 9-pan palette MOQ 5000.png`).
- 2026-11-02: technical drawings (PDF, not downloaded) for **YR3122A, YR3033G and YR3121A**.
- 2026-11-03: MOQ **10,000**; 2,000 possible but "slightly more expensive" (price not given). (The listing said 5,000.)
- 2026-11-03: **YR3121A 3x3 with mirror: 0.8 USD per piece, FOB Shenzhen**, without printing or other decoration. One-colour screen-printed logo +0.045 USD. Logo and custom container colour possible.
- 2026-11-03: YR3121A well size from their drawing: **21.9 ±0.15 mm at the top, 21.6 ±0.15 mm at the bottom, 4.2 mm deep**; case 79 mm wide (`2026-11-03 YR3121A section drawing wells 21.9 mm.png`). Lacy thinks a 22x22 pan "should" fit; by the numbers it is 0.1-0.4 mm too small, and a filled pan is slightly larger ([[Packaging]]).
- 2026-11-03: samples available (cost not given).
- 2026-11-03: YR3033G has a slightly larger (round) pan but is not a 3x3 or 3x4 layout. They also make single-well compacts; Greg said 4x2 would also work for him.
- 2026-11-03: the 9-grid listing Greg sent (id 1601651375852, MOQ 12,000) is the same as YR3122 with a cover plate.
- 2026-11-04: **YR3130C**: 4 wells (2x2), 63.8 x 73 x 13.1 mm, filling 1.95 ml; section drawing shows wells **25 ±0.2 mm** (24.7 at the bottom), 4.5 mm deep. Lacy: "maybe meets 25x25 pan" (`2026-11-07 YR3130C 4 wells 63.8x73x13.1 mm.png`, `2026-11-07 section drawing 4 wells 25 mm.png`).
- 2026-11-04: a custom grid with 22.5 mm wells means paying for a new mould, **about 4,000-5,000 USD**; Lacy advises against it.
- 2026-11-05: **YR3091D**: 8 round wells (4x2), 140 x 85 x 11.8 mm, filling 2.3 ml, inner well diameter **27 mm**, mirror (`2026-11-07 YR3091D 8 round wells 140x85x11.8 mm.png`).
- 2026-11-05/06: **YR3091** also comes with a square-well tray: 8 wells in 4x2, **22.5 ±0.2 mm at the top, 22.3 ±0.2 mm at the bottom**, about 4.9 mm overall depth on the drawing; case 140 x 85 mm; no brush slot (Lacy, 2026-11-06). QC spec on the drawing: opening force 0.2-1.2 kgf, mirror removal ≥3 kgf, tray-base separation ≥4 kgf (`2026-11-07 YR3091 drawing 8 square wells 22.5 mm.png`). Full drawing: YR3091F PDF, not downloaded.
- 2026-11-09: no version with shallower wells ("sorry, no others").
