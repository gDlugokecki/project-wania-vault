# Boolong Packaging

Ningbo Boolong Packaging Co., Ltd., China. One of the [[Case supplier]] candidates for the [[Packaging]], contacted by Greg on 2026-10-29 on Alibaba.

## Snapshot

- **Status:** no fit: 2,000 is "much lower" than their MOQ (2026-11-03); PC7037 MOQ 10,000
- **Where:** Ningbo, China
- **Contact:** Mia Shen, Alibaba chat
- **Next step:** none

## What they can provide

As they state it, 2026-10-30:

- **PC7037-A** and **PC7037-B**, 3x3 palettes (B has an extra top piece). Snap-on lid, **not magnetic**. ABS. Mirror in the lid (photos).
- Drawing: 90 mm square, 13 mm high, **9 square wells on a 24 mm grid**, body and cover ABS, "2g*9" ([[raw/suppliers/boolong-packaging/2026-10-30 PC7037 drawing 9 square wells 24 mm.png|2026-10-30 PC7037 drawing 9 square wells 24 mm.png]]). If the wells are about 24 mm inside, 22x22 mm pans would likely fit, 25x25 mm would not; the drawing does not say whether 24 mm is the well or the pitch.
- Sample photos with their decoration (`2026-10-30 PC7037-A samples.png`, `2026-10-30 PC7037-B samples.png`): printed, metallic gold and silver, black, pastel colours.
- Cosmetic catalog PDF (9.52 MB), not downloaded.
- 2026-11-02: MOQ for PC7037 is 12,000 (complex process), lowered to **10,000** for a first order. Greg had said 3,000-5,000.
