**Subject:** Inquiry: empty eyeshadow palettes (9/12 pans), quotation and samples

Hello,

My name is Grzegorz Długokęcki and I am launching a new eyeshadow brand based in Poland (EU). The brand name will be announced soon. I am looking for a manufacturer of empty eyeshadow palettes and would like to request a quotation and samples. I am looking for an existing (stock) palette mold that can be customised with my logo and a custom color (Pantone). I do not plan to pay for a new mold.

Product specification:
- Layout: 9 shades (3x3) or 12 shades (3x4). Please quote both options.
- Pans: round Ø26 mm, or square / rectangular 22x22 mm, 25x25 mm or 23x20 mm. Please tell me which of these sizes your existing palettes can take. Pans will be filled and glued into the palette by my eyeshadow supplier, so the palettes will be delivered empty.
- Well fitting: the metal pan stretches slightly when the eyeshadow is pressed, so the wells must fit filled pans, not empty ones. Please tell me which of your existing palettes fit the pan sizes above. I can send filled sample pans to check the fit.
- Mirror: large, good-quality mirror covering the inside of the lid, securely attached.
- Closure: magnetic closure preferred. Please also suggest other options you offer.
- Material: high-quality, sturdy, non-transparent plastic. Please recommend suitable materials (e.g. ABS, AS, PETG) and the price difference between them.
- Finish: custom color (Pantone). Please list the finishes you offer (e.g. matte, semi-matte, metallic).
- Logo decoration: please list available options (hot stamping, silk screen, embossing).
- Estimated first order: 1,000–2,000 palettes.

Could you please let me know:
1. MOQ and unit prices for 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000 pcs.
2. Which of your existing molds fit these pan sizes (please send photos and well dimensions). For comparison only: the cost of a custom mold.
3. Samples of your existing palettes: could you send me a few samples so I can check the quality of the plastic, the mirror and the closure? Please let me know the cost and delivery time to Poland.
4. Custom samples: what is the cost and lead time for samples with my logo and color, with wells fitted to our filled pans?
5. Lead time from design approval: how long does it take from my approval of the final design to (a) the pre-production sample and (b) finished mass production?
6. Shipping options to Poland (EXW / FOB / DDP).
7. Payment terms.
8. Quality control process and defect tolerance (AQL).
9. Material documentation for the EU market (material specification, REACH compliance).
10. Whether you can produce an outer paper box as well.
11. Recyclable or recycled-plastic options.

I would like to receive samples first to check the quality before placing an order.

Thank you, and I look forward to your reply.

Best regards,
Grzegorz Długokęcki
